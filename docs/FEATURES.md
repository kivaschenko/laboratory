## 🧪 Substance Management

### Substance Catalog
![Substances List](../screenshots/substances-list.png)

The substance catalog manages your chemical inventory with:
- **Complete substance database** with CAS numbers and properties
- **Real-time stock levels** with automatic calculations
- **Cost tracking** per unit and total inventory value
- **Search and filtering** capabilities for large inventories

### Adding New Substances
![Add Substance](../screenshots/add-substance.png)

Simple form-based substance registration:
- **Chemical properties** including concentration and purity
- **Measurement units** with automatic conversions
- **Cost information** for accurate analysis pricing
- **Safety classifications** and handling requirements
